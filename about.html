MILD AI

An AI assistant you can just talk to.

MILD AI is a free AI companion for chat, images, files and code. Ask questions, get help writing, create images and work through code, in a simple chat that works in your browser and as an Android app.

Open MILD AI

What you can do

  • Ask anything and get a written answer in seconds.
  • Get help drafting, rewriting and summarising text.
  • Get help understanding, writing and fixing code.
  • Generate images from a text description (Photo Gen).
  • Attach files to a chat and ask questions about them.
  • Keep several conversations and come back to them later, saved on your own device.

Why sign-in is needed

You create a MILD AI account with email, or continue with Google or GitHub. We use your name and email address only to set up your account and keep you signed in. We do not use them for advertising, and we do not sell them.

How your messages are handled

Messages you send are passed to Google's Gemini AI service to produce replies. Your chat history is stored on your own device. You can read the details in our Privacy Policy.
