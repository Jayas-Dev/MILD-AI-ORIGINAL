Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

MILD AI is an AI chat assistant available as a website and as an Android app. This page explains what information it handles and why.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign up or sign in with email, Google or GitHub, we receive your email address, your name (if you give one or your provider shares it) and a unique account ID. This is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. Passwords are never visible to us.
  • Your chats. Conversations, settings and preferences are saved in storage on your own device (your browser, or the app on Android). We do not keep a copy of them on our servers.
  • Usage statistics. The app uses Firebase Analytics, which collects basic anonymous information such as pages viewed, device type and approximate region.

How your messages are processed

When you send a message that needs an AI reply, its text (and the recent conversation, so the answer makes sense) is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the response. Please do not share passwords, bank details or other sensitive information in chats. Google handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy.

How we use information

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To generate replies to your messages.
  • To understand how the app is used and to fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

Firebase (Google) for sign-in and analytics, Google Gemini for AI replies, and GitHub Pages for hosting this site. Each has its own privacy policy.

Sign in with Google or GitHub

If you use these options, we only read your basic profile: your name and email address. We do not access your files, contacts or repositories.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time from inside the app.
  • Clearing the app's data (Android settings) or your browser's site data removes your saved chats and settings from your device.
  • To have your account deleted, email us at the address below from the email used on your account.

Children

MILD AI is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
